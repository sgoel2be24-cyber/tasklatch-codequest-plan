## Evidence excluded from headline claims

The viral Clawdbot $2,997 purchase was traced to an author's social post, but independent transaction proof or a technical postmortem was not established. Do not use it as verified loss evidence. Do not combine broad AI breach averages with agent incidents to manufacture an average loss.

## Local observations

Earlier read-only checks reported an Apple M5 MacBook Air with 16 GB memory, about 35 GiB free on the relevant filesystem, installed Docker/Colima clients, and Colima stopped. This is an environment snapshot, not a two-worker performance test. No personal hardware identifiers are retained here.

The E2B account screenshot is user-provided evidence of this workspace's block. Do not place the screenshot containing account details into public slides or repository history. Keep only the operational conclusion needed for the architecture decision.

## Novelty and market claims still requiring validation

The project name has had only a lightweight web collision check, not trademark clearance. OpenShell's policy coverage is confirmed, but its integration fit and relative workflow usability still need testing. The target user's pain, workflow frequency, adoption willingness, and actual reduction in review effort are hypotheses to investigate with users after the prototype works.
